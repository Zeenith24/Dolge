Title: Kitchen Disaster

I was trying to make breakfast this morning when everything went wrong.
I grabbed what I thought was the sugar container but it was actually salt.
I added a whole spoonful of salt to my pancake batter.
When I tasted it, I nearly spit it out - it was incredibly salty!
I had to start over and this time I double-checked the containers.
Moral of the story: always label your containers clearly!
